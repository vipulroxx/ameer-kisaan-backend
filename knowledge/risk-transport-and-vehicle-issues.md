# Transport and vehicle issues

Regular maintenance is crucial for operational efficiency.
- Observed: Over 30% of vehicles show signs of neglect

## Fuel Efficiency
Monitoring fuel usage can reduce costs significantly.
- Observed: Poor fuel efficiency impacts profitability

## Driver Safety
Ensuring safety practices among drivers reduces accidents.
- Observed: Accidents lead to delays and increased expenses

## Logistics Optimization
Efficient routing can save time and fuel.
