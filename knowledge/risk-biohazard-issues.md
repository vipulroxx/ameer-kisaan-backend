# Biohazard issues

Chemical runoff affecting water quality.

Diseases spreading due to unsanitary conditions.
- Observed: Recent outbreaks of crop pathogens impacting yields

Non-native species harming local crops.
- Observed: Invasive species observed in over 30% of local farms

## Animal Waste Management
Improper disposal leading to health hazards.
- Observed: High levels of waste near crop areas detected
