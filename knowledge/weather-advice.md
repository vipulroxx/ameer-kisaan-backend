# Weather and field advice

## General cultivation tips
- Water your crops early in the morning to reduce evaporation.
- Rotate your crops each season to improve soil health.
- Use organic fertilizers to enhance crop growth.
- Monitor for pests regularly to prevent infestations.

## Frost and freeze alerts
- When frost risk is high, take protective measures for your crops (cover seedlings, irrigate lightly the evening before, avoid pruning).

## Heat stress management
- When the current temperature is above 30 °C, make sure crops are adequately hydrated.
- Below that, temperature is manageable; keep monitoring closely.

## Humidity
- Humidity above 80% can lead to fungal diseases. Monitor crops closely for leaf spots, mildew and rot.

## Irrigation
- Soil moisture below 30% is low: consider irrigating your crops.
- If rainfall is expected soon, adjust irrigation plans accordingly to avoid waterlogging.

## Rainfall
- If no significant rainfall is expected in the coming days, plan irrigation and mulching to conserve soil moisture.
