# Bribery and corruption issues

## Corruption in Land Acquisition
Bribes may be needed to secure land deals.
- Observed: 20% of farmers report issues with land acquisition

## Government Subsidy Manipulation
Bribery can affect the distribution of subsidies.
- Observed: 50% of subsidy applications are influenced by corruption

## License Procurement
Obtaining licenses can be a lengthy process without bribes.
- Observed: Long delays reported in license processing

## Market Access Barriers
Bribes may be required to access certain markets.
- Observed: 30% of farmers face barriers due to bribery
