# Wildfire risks

## High Temperatures
Increased temperatures leading to dryer conditions.
- Observed: Average temperatures rose by 2°C over the last decade

## Poor Vegetation Management
Neglected areas can contribute to increased fire risks.
- Observed: Over 30% of forests are at risk due to lack of management

## Wind Patterns
Strong winds can exacerbate wildfire spread.
- Observed: Windy conditions reported to spread fires rapidly

## Human Activities
Negligence in outdoor activities can trigger wildfires.
- Observed: 60% of wildfires are caused by human activities
