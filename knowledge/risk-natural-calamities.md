# Natural calamities

## Drought
Insufficient rainfall leading to water scarcity.
- Observed: 50% of farmers report crop loss due to drought

## Flood
Excess rainfall causing inundation of fields.
- Observed: 40% of farmers face losses due to floods

## Cyclones
High winds and rains that can destroy crops.
- Observed: 25% of regions affected by cyclones

## Earthquakes
Can cause damage to infrastructure affecting farming.
- Observed: 15% of farmers report issues due to earthquakes
