# Forest management issues

## Deforestation
Loss of forest cover due to logging and agriculture.
- Observed: Forests lost at a rate of 13 million hectares annually

## Invasive Species
Non-native species that disrupt local ecosystems.
- Observed: Invasive species cause significant biodiversity loss

## Forest Fires
Uncontrolled fires leading to destruction of forests.
- Observed: Over 4 million hectares affected by wildfires each year

## Illegal Logging
Unregulated cutting down of trees affecting forest sustainability.
- Observed: Estimated 15-30% of global timber is harvested illegally
