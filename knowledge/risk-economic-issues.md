# Economic issues

## Loan Burden
High interest rates affecting small farmers.
- Observed: Avg. 20% interest rate

## Crop Prices
Market fluctuations impact farmer income.
- Observed: Price decreased by 15% this year

## Market Access
Limited access to profitable markets.
- Observed: Distance from markets: 20km

## Weather Damage
Seasonal monsoons damage crops.
- Observed: High risk of loss due to rains
