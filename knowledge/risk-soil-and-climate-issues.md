# Soil and climate issues

## Soil Erosion
Loss of fertile topsoil affecting crop productivity.
- Observed: 40% of arable land affected by erosion

## Soil Salinity
High salt concentration reducing soil fertility.
- Observed: Salinity levels increased by 30% this season

## Nutrient Depletion
Over-farming leading to nutrient loss in soil.
- Observed: Nutrient levels dropped by 50%

## Soil Compaction
Heavy machinery damaging soil structure.
- Observed: Compaction levels have risen significantly

## pH Imbalance
Inadequate soil pH affecting nutrient absorption.
- Observed: pH levels vary between 5.0 to 8.5
