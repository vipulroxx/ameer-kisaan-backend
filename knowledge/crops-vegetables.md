# Vegetables

A diverse range of vegetables that provide essential nutrients and flavors to your meals.

## Benefits
- Rich in vitamins and minerals
- Boosts immunity and digestion
- Low in calories for weight management

## General growing tips
- Plant in well-drained soil to avoid rot
- Water regularly but avoid overwatering
- Incorporate organic fertilizers for healthier plants

## Tomatoes
- Soil: Loamy soil with pH 6.0 - 6.8
- Watering: 1-2 inches per week
- Sunlight: Full sun (6-8 hours/day)
- Notes: Stake plants to prevent sprawling and encourage upright growth.

### Tomatoes growth timeline
- Planting (Day 1): Sow seeds in starter pots indoors.
- Germination (Day 7-14): Seedlings begin to emerge.
- Transplanting (Day 21-30): Move seedlings to outdoor garden beds.
- Flowering (Day 45-60): Flowers appear, indicating fruit development.
- Fruit Development (Day 60-90): Tomatoes start to form and ripen.
- Harvest (Day 90-120): Tomatoes are ripe and ready for picking.

## Onions
- Soil: Well-drained, fertile soil with pH 6.0 - 7.0
- Watering: 1 inch per week
- Sunlight: Full sun (6 hours/day)
- Notes: Space bulbs 4-6 inches apart to promote growth.

### Onions growth timeline
- Planting (Day 1): Sow onion seeds or sets directly in the ground.
- Germination (Day 14-21): Sprouts start to emerge, signaling growth.
- Bulb Development (Day 60-90): Onions develop into mature bulbs.
- Harvest (Day 90-120): Onions are ready for harvest when tops start to fall over.

## Carrots
- Soil: Loose, sandy loam with pH 6.0 - 6.8
- Watering: 1 inch per week
- Sunlight: Full sun (6-8 hours/day)
- Notes: Thin seedlings for proper spacing to allow roots to develop.

### Carrots growth timeline
- Planting (Day 1): Sow seeds directly in well-prepared soil.
- Germination (Day 14-21): Seedlings will emerge after 2-3 weeks.
- Growth (Day 30-60): Roots grow longer and develop sweetness.
- Harvest (Day 70-80): Carrots are ready to be pulled from the ground.

## BellPeppers
- Soil: Well-drained soil with pH 6.0 - 6.8
- Watering: 1-2 inches per week
- Sunlight: Full sun (6-8 hours/day)
- Notes: Best grown in warm weather, protect from cold snaps.

## Cucumbers
- Soil: Rich, well-drained soil with pH 6.0 - 6.8
- Watering: 1 inch per week
- Sunlight: Full sun (6-8 hours/day)
- Notes: Provide a trellis for climbing varieties to maximize space.
